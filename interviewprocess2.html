Our Interview Process

A step-by-step guide to your application journey with us.

1

Stage 1: Recruiter Screen

🕒 Approx. 30 Minutes

This initial discussion is focused on understanding your career aspirations, background, and ensuring alignment with the role's requirements. It's a great opportunity for you to ask questions about our company culture and the position details.

2

Stage 2: Hiring Manager Interview

🕒 Approx. 45 Minutes

You will meet directly with the hiring manager to dive deeper into your technical and soft skills. This stage is behavioral and competency-based, examining how your past experiences directly relate to the challenges of the role.

3

Stage 3: Assessment of Key Skills

🕒 Approx. 1 Hour

The final stage is a practical assessment, which may involve a take-home task, a live technical challenge, or a portfolio review, allowing you to demonstrate the core skills essential for success in this position.

What Happens Next?

Once the final stage is complete, our team will review all feedback and aim to provide a decision or final update within 3-5 business days. We appreciate your patience and interest in joining our team!
